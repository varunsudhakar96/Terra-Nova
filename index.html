The series

New ground. Same tribe.

Terra Nova is Latin for "new land". It's Mvmnt Tribe's trek series: every edition takes the tribe somewhere new. Edition 01 starts in the Nilgiris.

A day of raw adventure and quiet moments in the Nilgiris. Walk through rolling grasslands and old shola forest, and leave the city behind for a while.

  • Government-approved trek
  • 20 km round trip through the Nilgiris
  • Ancient shola forests and rolling grasslands
  • A visit to the remote Mukurthi Hut
  • Misty mountain landscapes and scenic trails
  • The biodiversity of the Western Ghats
  • Time away from city life
  • A trek with the Mvmnt Tribe community

Itinerary

Two days

Day 1COIMBATORE → OOTY

The climb up

  1. Meet at the pickup point in Coimbatore and drive up to Ooty through the winding ghat roads.
  2. Stop for breakfast or refreshments on the way if needed.
  3. Check in at the homestay and meet the rest of the group.
  4. Pre-trek briefing on the trail, safety and fitness.
  5. Dinner, then an early night. Overnight stay in Ooty.
Day 2PARSONS VALLEY

Trek day

  1. Early start. Drive from Ooty to the Parsons Valley trek base.
  2. Reporting and forest entry formalities. Meet the authorised forest guide.
  3. Trek through shola forest and grassland to Mukurthi Hut, with hydration and rest breaks along the way.
  4. Packed lunch at a permitted spot, depending on trail conditions.
  5. Return by the same route and report back at the base.
  6. Drive back to Coimbatore, arriving at night depending on conditions.

Dates and the final reporting and return times will be confirmed before departure. The plan depends on forest department permission, authorised guide arrangements, weather and trail conditions.

Fitness
matters

This is a tough trek: around 20 km and 8 hours of walking in one day. It is not recommended as a first trek for anyone without good walking endurance.

Leave no trace. Carry back all your personal waste and treat the forest with respect.
